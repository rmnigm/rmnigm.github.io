Research

A Machine-Learning Approach to Quantum State Estimation

Roman Nigmatullin, Nikolay Borisov, Bella Meretukova, David Kagramanyan, Andrey Tayduganov, Artem Ryzhikov, Denis Derkach

Physics of Particles and Nuclei, 56(6), 2025 · Oral at MMCP 2024

Paper / Code / Slides

We forecast quantum bit error rate (QBER) in quantum key distribution so that LDPC code rates can be chosen ahead of error correction. On real transmitter data, gradient-boosted exponential smoothing outperforms deep neural networks.
